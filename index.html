Good manners

It knows when to leave you alone.

A break you didn't ask for is just another interruption. So Still is picky about when it shows up.

  • Only for the chat you're looking at.other chats never trigger it
  • Half-written message? It waits.drafts pause breaks
  • Typing, clicking or scrolling resets the count.8 idle seconds
  • Switch chats or apps and it's gone.right away
  • Your AI needs permission? You're back.no missed prompts
  • Click or press Esc to skip.for that request

Get Still

A few minutes of setup. Then just wait.

Still is a small Mac beta. Automatic breaks are new, so expect a few rough edges.

  1. Open the download and drag Still into Applications.
  2. Open Still, then choose Setup & connections from the half-circle in your menu bar.
  3. Allow Still in Accessibility settings. That's how it knows which chat is on screen.
  4. Connect Claude, Codex or both, then start a new chat.
